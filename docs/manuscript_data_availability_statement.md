# Manuscript availability of data and materials statement

The datasets analyzed during the current study are publicly available. Xenium spatial transcriptomic data are available from the Gene Expression Omnibus under accession GSE300147. Visium spatial transcriptomic datasets are available from the Gene Expression Omnibus under accessions GSE281978 and GSE181300. Bulk transcriptomic survival data are available from TCGA-HNSC via cBioPortal, PanCancer Atlas 2018.

Custom code used for preprocessing, cell-type annotation and validation, nerve-associated cell scoring, spatial zone assignment, differential expression analysis, signature scoring, survival analysis, and figure generation is available at https://github.com/riyac105/hnscc-pni-spatial-transcriptomics and archived at Zenodo under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21997514. Raw datasets are not redistributed in this repository and should be obtained from their original public repositories.
